Aegis ID

This is the toy Identity Provider from auth-from-scratch. It plays the role Okta, Auth0, or any OIDC-compliant provider plays in a real system: it holds user credentials, authenticates users, and issues signed identity tokens to apps it trusts — without ever handing those apps a password.
